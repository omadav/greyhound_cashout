Workflow Pilot

This browser prototype tests the full task flow: dog choice, hidden assignment, confidence rating, race playback, mid-race cash-out, outcome feedback, and post-race motivation ratings.

The current pilot reuses one demo video and is meant for interface testing, not formal data collection. The full study will need converted web-ready race videos and a finalized stimulus schedule.

  • You will complete 4 short demo trials.
  • One trial includes a practice-style walkthrough of the cash-out flow.
  • Your responses are stored in the browser until you download them at the end.
