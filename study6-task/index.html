Study title: Decisions and outcomes in greyhound races

Researcher: Omar David Pérez R., Department of Industrial Engineering, Faculty of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, Universidad de Chile (omar.perez.r@uchile.cl)

Ethics committee: Ethics and Biosafety Committee for Research, Faculty of Physical and Mathematical Sciences (FCFM), Universidad de Chile

Please read the following before deciding whether to take part. If you have questions, you can contact the researcher before deciding.

What does this study involve?

This study investigates how people make decisions in a task based on greyhound races. You will be asked to choose a greyhound, watch short races, make decisions during the task, and answer a few short questions after each result. At the end of the task you will complete a brief questionnaire about gambling habits.

How long does it take?

The full session takes about 20–25 minutes.

What will be recorded?

The task records your responses, reaction times, and decisions during the task.

Are there any risks?

Risk is minimal. You never risk your own money. Some questions in the final questionnaire refer to past gambling habits, which could cause mild, temporary discomfort. If needed, you will be given information about support resources at the end of the session.

Information at the end of the session

At the end of the task you will receive a full explanation of the purpose of the study and some aspects of the design that were not shared beforehand, in order to preserve the validity of the results.

Confidentiality

Your data will be anonymised and stored securely. No results are communicated to individual participants. General findings may be published in scientific journals or presented at academic conferences. Anonymised data may be deposited in an open-access public repository at the time of publication.

Voluntary participation

Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may withdraw at any time without needing to give a reason and without any negative consequences for your pay for the time you have completed.

Compensation

You will receive a base payment through Prolific plus a bonus. You never risk your own money.

Session summary

About the study

We are studying how having the option to bet affects confidence and responses to race outcomes. Participants completed a version with betting or a version without betting, assigned at random.

The videos were recorded races. Dog names and their assignments to traps were arranged by the task so that everyone experienced a planned mix of clear wins, narrow wins, near misses, and clear losses. Your choice of name did not determine the outcome. We explain this now so that knowing it in advance would not affect your responses.

If you would like to discuss your participation, contact the researcher using the details above. If you would like support with gambling concerns, visit GamCare.
